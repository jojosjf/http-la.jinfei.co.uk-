FAQ

How do I measure a card?
Choose a photo of your card. The app detects the card's outer edges and artwork border and places 8 guide lines: the outer lines (long dashes) on the card's edges and the inner lines (short dashes) on the artwork border. The left/right and top/bottom ratios and a reference grade appear below.

Auto detect is off. What can I do?
Drag the handles to adjust the lines (a magnifier helps), or tap a handle and use the arrows to move the line 1 pixel at a time. If the photo is tilted, tap "Rotate" to straighten it, then tap "Auto detect" again. Shooting straight-on with all four edges in frame gives the best results.

Where are my records stored?
Only on your device. They contain no photos and are never uploaded. They are removed when you delete the app.

How do I save the result image?
Tap "Image" → "Save image", then choose "Save Image" in the share sheet to save it to Photos, or share it directly.

Is the reference grade accurate?
It is calculated from published centering standards and is for reference only; it is not an actual grade. This app is not affiliated with any card grading company.

常见问题

怎么测量？
从相册选一张卡牌照片，App 会自动识别卡牌外边和图案边框，放好 8 条参考线。外框线（长虚线）对齐卡牌外边缘，内框线（短虚线）对齐图案边框，下方即显示左右、上下比例和参考等级。

自动识别不准怎么办？
拖动手柄调整参考线，拖动时有放大镜；点一下手柄可以选中该线，再用箭头每次移动 1 像素。照片拍歪时可以点“旋转”手动转正，再点“自动识别”重新找边。拍照时尽量正对卡牌、让四边完整入镜，识别会更准确。

测量记录存在哪里？
只存在您自己的设备上，不含照片，也不会上传。删除 App 后记录随之删除。

结果图怎么保存？
点“结果图”→“保存图片”，在系统分享面板中选择“存储图像”即可保存到“照片”，也可以直接分享。

参考等级准确吗？
参考等级依据公开的居中标准计算，仅供参考，不代表实际评级。本应用与任何卡牌评级机构均无关联。
