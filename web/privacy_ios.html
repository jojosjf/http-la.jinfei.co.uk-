Card Centering Check — Privacy Policy (iOS)

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Effective: 1 October 2026

Summary

Card Centering Check works entirely offline on your iPhone or iPad. We do not collect, upload or share any personal information, the app contains no third-party SDKs, and it does no tracking. Measurements you choose to save stay on your own device and never include photos.

Card Centering Check (“the app”) is developed and operated by [Developer name] (“we”). This policy follows applicable data protection laws and Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines. Please read it before using the app.

2. Information we collect

The app collects no personal information:

  • There is no sign-up or login and no user account;
  • It does not collect device identifiers (such as IDFA or IDFV), location, contacts, calendars or similar information;
  • It does no analytics, behavioural analysis, advertising or cross-app tracking;
  • It does not communicate with any server; photos and measurements never leave your device.

3. How your photo is used

  • You choose one card photo with the system photo picker. The app can read only that photo and cannot browse your photo library, so it does not need Photos access;
  • The photo is used only in memory on your device for display, measuring and edge detection. It is never uploaded or sent to anyone; edge detection runs entirely on the device;
  • The app does not save your photo. It is cleared from memory when you choose another photo or close the app.

4. Saved measurements

Only when you tap “Save” does the app store a measurement record, containing only: the left/right and top/bottom ratios, the reference grade, the time, and an optional note you type (such as the card name).

  • Records contain no photos and no device information;
  • Records are kept in the app’s private storage on your device and are never uploaded. If you use iCloud or computer backups, they are included in your device backup and handled by Apple under your backup settings; we cannot access them;
  • You can swipe left to delete a record or tap “Clear” to delete all records in Records. Deleting the app removes them.

5. Result image

The result image is created on your device and shows the card, guide lines, ratios and reference grade. Only when you tap “Save image” does the app open the system share sheet, where you choose “Save Image” to save it to Photos or share it. The first time you save, iOS asks whether to allow the app to add photos (write-only; it cannot read your library). The app never saves or shares the image on its own.

7. Third-party SDKs

The app contains no third-party SDKs, including advertising, analytics, push, login, payment, sharing and crash reporting SDKs. It is built with the open-source Flutter framework, which collects and transmits no data in this app.

8. Sharing and disclosure

As the app collects no personal information, we do not share, sell, transfer or disclose personal information to any company, organisation or individual.

9. Storage

The app stores nothing on any server and does not save your photos on the device. Measurements you choose to save stay on your device (see section 4). There is no cross-border transfer of personal information.

10. Your rights

As we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for you to request access to, correct or delete, and no account to close. You can delete records in the app or delete the app at any time; the app then leaves none of your data on the device (except result images you saved to Photos). If you believe your rights have been harmed, you can contact us or complain to the relevant authority.

11. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 14. Minors should read this policy and use the app with a parent or guardian.

12. Disclaimer

The reference grade is calculated from published centering standards and is for reference only; it is not an actual grade. The app is not affiliated with any card grading company.

13. Changes to this policy

If a change to the app affects how personal information is handled, we will update this policy and the date on this page. We will not reduce your rights under this policy without your explicit consent.

「卡牌居中检查」完全在您的 iPhone 或 iPad 上离线运行。我们不收集、不上传、不共享您的任何个人信息，不包含任何第三方 SDK，不进行任何追踪。您主动保存的测量记录只存在您自己的设备上，不含照片。

二、我们收集的信息

本应用不收集任何个人信息：

  • 不需要注册或登录，没有账号体系；
  • 不收集设备标识符（如 IDFA、IDFV）、位置、通讯录、日历等信息；
  • 不进行任何数据统计、行为分析、广告或跨 App 追踪；
  • 本应用不与任何服务器通信，照片和测量数据都不会离开您的设备。

三、照片的使用方式

  • 本应用通过系统照片选择器，由您主动选择一张卡牌照片。本应用只能读取您选中的那一张，无法浏览您的照片图库，因此不需要“照片”访问权限；
  • 照片只在设备内存中用于显示、测量和自动识别卡牌边框，不会上传到任何服务器，也不会发送给任何第三方；自动识别完全在设备上计算完成；
  • 本应用不保存您的照片。更换照片或退出应用后，之前的照片即从内存中清除。

四、测量记录

只有在您点击“保存”时，本应用才会保存一条测量记录，内容仅包括：左右和上下比例、参考等级、保存时间，以及您自愿填写的备注（如卡牌名称）。

  • 测量记录不包含照片，也不包含任何设备信息；
  • 记录只保存在本应用在您设备上的私有存储空间中，不会上传；如果您开启了 iCloud 或电脑备份，它会随设备备份一起由 Apple 按您的备份设置处理，我们无法访问；
  • 您可以在“记录”页左滑删除单条记录，或点“清空”删除全部记录；删除本应用后，记录随之删除。

五、结果图

结果图由本应用在设备上生成，包含卡牌图片、参考线、比例和参考等级。只有在您点击“保存图片”时，本应用才会打开系统分享面板，由您选择“存储图像”保存到“照片”，或分享给他人。首次存储时，系统会询问是否允许本应用添加照片（仅写入，不能读取您的照片图库）。本应用不会自动保存或分享结果图。

本应用不在服务器上存储任何信息，也不在设备上保存您的照片。您主动保存的测量记录只存于您的设备上（见第四条）。不涉及个人信息的跨境传输。

十、您的权利
