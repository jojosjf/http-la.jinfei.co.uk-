简要说明

「卡牌居中检查」完全在您的 iPhone 或 iPad 上离线运行。我们不收集、不上传、不共享您的任何个人信息，不包含任何第三方 SDK，不进行任何追踪。您主动保存的测量记录只存在您自己的设备上，不含照片。

二、我们收集的信息

本应用不收集任何个人信息：

  • 不需要注册或登录，没有账号体系；
  • 不收集设备标识符（如 IDFA、IDFV）、位置、通讯录、日历等信息；
  • 不进行任何数据统计、行为分析、广告或跨 App 追踪；
  • 本应用不与任何服务器通信，照片和测量数据都不会离开您的设备。

三、照片的使用方式

  • 本应用通过系统照片选择器，由您主动选择一张卡牌照片。本应用只能读取您选中的那一张，无法浏览您的照片图库，因此不需要“照片”访问权限；
  • 照片只在设备内存中用于显示、测量和自动识别卡牌边框，不会上传到任何服务器，也不会发送给任何第三方；自动识别完全在设备上计算完成；
  • 本应用不保存您的照片。更换照片或退出应用后，之前的照片即从内存中清除。

四、测量记录

只有在您点击“保存”时，本应用才会保存一条测量记录，内容仅包括：左右和上下比例、参考等级、保存时间，以及您自愿填写的备注（如卡牌名称）。

  • 测量记录不包含照片，也不包含任何设备信息；
  • 记录只保存在本应用在您设备上的私有存储空间中，不会上传；如果您开启了 iCloud 或电脑备份，它会随设备备份一起由 Apple 按您的备份设置处理，我们无法访问；
  • 您可以在“记录”页左滑删除单条记录，或点“清空”删除全部记录；删除本应用后，记录随之删除。

五、结果图

结果图由本应用在设备上生成，包含卡牌图片、参考线、比例和参考等级。只有在您点击“保存图片”时，本应用才会打开系统分享面板，由您选择“存储图像”保存到“照片”，或分享给他人。首次存储时，系统会询问是否允许本应用添加照片（仅写入，不能读取您的照片图库）。本应用不会自动保存或分享结果图。

Card Centering Check — Privacy Policy (iOS)

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Summary

Card Centering Check works entirely offline on your iPhone or iPad. We do not collect, upload or share any personal information, the app contains no third-party SDKs, and it does no tracking. Measurements you choose to save stay on your device and never include photos.

1. Information we collect

None. There is no sign-up or login. The app does not collect device identifiers (such as IDFA or IDFV), location, contacts or any other personal information, does no analytics, advertising or cross-app tracking, and does not communicate with any server.

2. Photos

You choose one photo with the system photo picker; the app can read only that photo and cannot browse your library, so it does not need Photos access. The photo is used only in memory on your device for display, measuring and edge detection. It is never uploaded, shared or saved by the app.

3. Saved measurements

Only when you tap “Save”, the app stores the ratios, reference grade, time and an optional note you type. Records contain no photos or device information and stay in the app’s private storage on your device; they may be included in your iCloud or computer backup under your own backup settings, which we cannot access. You can delete records in the app at any time, and they are removed when you delete the app.

4. Result image

The result image is created on your device. When you tap “Save image”, the system share sheet opens and you choose to save it to Photos or share it. The first time you save, iOS asks whether to allow the app to add photos (write-only). The app never saves or shares the image on its own.

5. Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs. It is built with the open-source Flutter framework, which collects and transmits no data in this app. We do not share, sell or disclose any personal information.

6. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Disclaimer

The reference grade is calculated from published centering standards and is for reference only; it is not an actual grade. The app is not affiliated with any card grading company.

8. Changes and contact

If a change to the app affects how information is handled, we will update this page and its date. For any questions, contact [Developer name] at [Contact email]; we will reply within 15 working days.
