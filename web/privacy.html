Card Centering Check — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Effective: 28 September 2026

Summary

Card Centering Check works entirely offline on your device. We do not collect, upload, store or share any personal information. The app requests no device permissions and contains no third-party SDKs.

Card Centering Check (“the app”) is developed and operated by [Developer name] (“we”). We take your personal information seriously, and this policy follows applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Law of the People’s Republic of China. Please read it before using the app.

1. Information we collect

The app collects no personal information. In particular, it:

  • requires no sign-up or login and has no user accounts;
  • does not collect device identifiers (such as IMEI, OAID, Android ID or MAC address);
  • does not collect location, contacts, call logs, messages or similar information;
  • does no analytics, behavioural analysis or advertising tracking.

2. How your photo is used

The app measures how well a card’s borders are centered in a photo. To do this:

  • You choose one photo with the system photo picker. The app can read only the photo you choose and cannot browse your library;
  • The photo is used only in memory on your device for display and measuring. It is never uploaded to any server or sent to any third party;
  • Some systems copy the chosen photo into the app’s temporary folder; the app deletes that copy right after reading it and never keeps your photo;
  • Measurements are only shown on screen and are not recorded or saved. When you close the page or choose another photo, the previous photo and measurements are cleared from memory.

3. Device permissions

The released app requests no device permissions, including network, storage, camera and photo library access.

PlatformPermissions requestedNotes

4. Third-party SDKs

The app contains no third-party SDKs, including advertising, analytics, push, login, payment and crash reporting SDKs. It is built with the open-source Flutter framework, which collects and transmits no data in this app.

5. Sharing, transfer and disclosure

As the app collects no personal information, we do not share, transfer or publicly disclose personal information to any company, organisation or individual.

6. Storage

The app stores no personal information or photos on your device or on any server, and there is no cross-border transfer of personal information. The Android version keeps one setting on the device — whether you have agreed to this policy — so the notice is not shown every time. It contains no personal information and is removed when you uninstall the app.

7. Web version

If you use the web version, your photo is likewise processed only in your browser and never uploaded to a server. The web host may keep routine access logs (such as access time, IP address and browser type) only to keep the site running and secure; these logs are not linked to your photos.

8. Your rights

As we neither collect nor store your personal information, there is nothing for you to request access to, correct or delete, and no account to close. You can uninstall the app at any time, and it leaves none of your data on the device.

9. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 14. Minors should read this policy and use the app with a parent or guardian.

10. Disclaimer

The grade shown by the app is based only on published grading standards and is for reference only. The app is not affiliated with any card grading company.

11. Changes to this policy

If a change to the app affects how personal information is handled, we will update this policy and tell you prominently in the app, for example with a notice. We will not reduce your rights under this policy without your explicit consent.

一、我们收集的信息

本应用不收集任何个人信息。具体来说：

  • 不需要注册或登录，没有账号体系；
  • 不收集设备标识符（如 IMEI、OAID、Android ID、MAC 地址等）；

二、照片的使用方式

本应用的核心功能是测量卡牌照片的边框居中度。为此：

  • 本应用通过系统自带的照片选择器，由您主动选择一张照片。本应用只能读取您选中的那一张照片，无法浏览您的相册；
  • 照片只在设备内存中用于显示和测量，不会上传到任何服务器，也不会发送给任何第三方；
  • 部分系统在选图时会把照片复制一份到本应用的临时目录，本应用读取后会立即删除这份临时副本，不会保存您的照片；
  • 测量结果只显示在屏幕上，不会被记录或保存。关闭页面或更换照片后，之前的照片和测量数据即从内存中清除。

本应用正式版不申请任何设备权限，包括网络权限、存储权限、相机权限和相册读取权限。
